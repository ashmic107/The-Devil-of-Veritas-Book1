The Story

Killian Vane came to Veritas University with a stolen scholarship and a new name — desperate to escape the wreckage of his past. What he didn't expect was Dante Moretti — the cold, devastatingly beautiful heir to the most feared crime syndicate on the East Coast. When Killian stumbles into a secret blood ritual deep beneath the library, Dante doesn't kill him. He does something far more dangerous: he claims him. Now Killian is Dante's shadow, his hostage, his obsession — and slowly, terrifyingly, his salvation. But the truth buried in Dante's past could destroy everything. Will he be Killian's salvation — or his downfall?

First Chapter Preview

Step Into Veritas

The gates of Veritas University didn't swing open — they groaned, like an ancient beast admitting a meal it didn't particularly want to swallow.

I gripped the strap of my duffel bag until my knuckles turned white. I was twenty-one, a transfer student with a scholarship I'd lied to get and a name I was still getting used to.

Killian Vane. It sounded sharper than the name I'd left behind in the trailers of Ohio.

Then, the air changed. It wasn't a sound. It was a shift in pressure. The crowd parted — a silent, rhythmic movement that reminded me of schooling fish sensing a shark.

Dante Moretti. He was tall, dressed in a charcoal suit that probably cost more than my life insurance policy. His hair was black, slicked back with lethal precision, framing a face that was hauntingly beautiful and entirely hollow.

"You're in the wrong place, little bird," he said. His voice was a low, melodic rasp that sent a shiver straight down my spine. It wasn't a greeting. It was a verdict.

— Chapter 1: The Lion's Den
